UCCJEA Information
Understanding the UCCJEA (Uniform Child Custody Jurisdiction and Enforcement Act)

The UCCJEA is a law that helps determine which state has jurisdiction to make decisions about child custody. It ensures that only one state makes these decisions at a time, to avoid conflicts and ensure consistency.

What You Need to Report:
  • Child's Residence: Every place your child has lived in the past five years
  • Adults in home: Names and addresses of all persons the child lived with during that time
  • Other Custody Claims: Any custody proceedings involving your child, past or present
  • Knowledge of Other Proceedings: Any other person who might have custody or visitation rights, or claims to have them

Why Accuracy is Critical: Your affidavit is a sworn statement. Inaccurate or incomplete information can delay proceedings, result in penalties for providing false information, and negatively impact the custody decision. Approximate dates are OK — provide your best estimates and be honest.
